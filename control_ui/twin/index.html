What is real?

Full-resolution official STL geometry, source filenames, mesh bounds and your recorded channel limits. Multi-piece print plates are separated; originals are retained without simplification.

What needs verification?

Skull mating surfaces, small linkage placement, servo pivots and differences in your printed revisions. Eye and eyelid movement is a visual approximation, not a collision-checked mechanism. No silicone skin or measured shaft feedback is included.
